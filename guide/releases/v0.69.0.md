# SimpleArchitecture 0.69.0

Lines between cards apart both across and up or down take one corner, into the top of the lower card.

**Still a beta.** Back up your database before you upgrade, and please [report what you find](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues).

## What changes

- **One corner where one will do.** A line between two cards that are apart both across and up or down now leaves the upper card by its side facing the other card and goes straight down into the top of the lower one, with one corner, in place of two corners into the lower card's side. It may cross a container's name. When another card is in that way, the line is drawn as before. Lines moved by hand keep their route; **Tidy relations** draws them again the new way. See [The canvas](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.69.0/guide/canvas.md) ([#39](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/39)).

## Upgrade

There are no database changes: start 0.69.0 in place of the older version. See [Hosting the image](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.69.0/guide/hosting.md) for upgrading in general.

## Known issues

- Widening a card can leave other lines running over it ([#47](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/47)).
