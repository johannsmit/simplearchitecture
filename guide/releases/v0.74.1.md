# SimpleArchitecture 0.74.1

Admin → Logs shows the right hours when the date range crosses a clock change.

**Still a beta.** Back up your database before you upgrade, and please [report what you find](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues).

## What changes

- **Logs over a clock change** ([#88](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/88)): in a time zone with summer time, a range whose **From** day was before the last clock change was shifted by an hour, so the first hour of that day was left out, or an hour of the day before was shown. Each end of the range now uses the time zone offset of its own date. See [Admin](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.74.1/guide/admin.md).

## Upgrade

There are no database changes: start 0.74.1 in place of the older version. See [Hosting the image](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.74.1/guide/hosting.md) for upgrading in general.

## Known issues

- Without Discard, an accidental edit is saved. An "undo the last change" is planned.
- Widening a card can leave other lines running over it ([#47](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/47)).
