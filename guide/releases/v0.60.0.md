# SimpleArchitecture 0.60.0 (beta)

The first public release of SimpleArchitecture, a web app for modelling your IT landscape. Everything in it is new.

**This is a beta.** The app does what these notes describe, but few people have used it so far. Expect rough edges, back up your database, and please [report what you find](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues).

## What it does

- **Model your landscape** as elements and relationships, against a small [default metamodel](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.60.0/metamodel/default-metamodel.md): Stakeholder (a person or an AI agent), Business, Application, Infrastructure and Other, linked by Relation and Contains.
- **Three spaces, three roles.** Viewers read every model in **Viewing**; Modellers also change elements, relationships and views in **Modelling**; Admins also manage the installation in **Admin**.
- **Explore on a canvas** centred on one element, with what it relates to up to three steps away, laid out for you. Containers are drawn with what they contain inside them. Move and widen cards, tidy the whole canvas or only its lines, and, in a view, move a line's bends by hand.
- **Save views**: named, stored sets of elements and relationships with their layout, grouped in folders. An element's details show which views it is in.
- **Show Viewpoints** chooses what the lines show in place of their labels: the architecture state, the identity used, the data that flows each way, or properties your Admin defined.
- **Find and list** elements by search and by property values, in the element list or the catalog: a sortable table per element type, which in Modelling you can edit in place and download as CSV.
- **Relationship properties** an Admin defines, as text or as a choice from a list, on every Relation.
- **Import and export:** import JSON Graph Format files and ArchiMate exchange files (OEF), with a mapping you can change; export any model as JSON Graph Format.
- **AI agents** such as Claude can read the models you can see, through the app's MCP endpoint, after you sign in and allow them. You see and disconnect them under **Connected apps**.
- **Your own look:** a logo and two colours for everyone, and a Light or Dark theme for yourself.
- **The keyboard:** most things work from the keyboard too.

## What it needs

- **A container host** for the image `ghcr.io/johannsmit/simplearchitecture:0.60.0`, on Intel/AMD (`linux/amd64`) or ARM (`linux/arm64`), with an https proxy in front.
- **PostgreSQL.** The app keeps everything there and sets up its tables itself when it starts. It connects with TLS and checks the server's certificate. A plain connection needs `?sslmode=disable` in `DATABASE_URL`, and reaches only the same machine or a host with a one-word name, such as `db` in the Compose example.
- **An OpenID Connect provider** that can send roles or groups in the ID token, such as Microsoft Entra ID. The app keeps no passwords and no roles of its own.
- **A desktop or laptop browser**, with a mouse and keyboard. Phones and tablets aren't supported.

## Get started

- [Run with Docker Compose](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.60.0/guide/docker-compose.md): the app and its own PostgreSQL on one machine, two containers and nothing else.
- [Hosting the image](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.60.0/guide/hosting.md) on any other container host, with its ten settings.
- [Sign-in and roles](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.60.0/guide/sign-in.md), with [Azure examples](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.60.0/guide/azure.md).
- To try it with made-up data, import `samples/sample-insurance.jgf.json` from the repository on **Admin → Models**.
- Everything else is in [the guide](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.60.0/guide/README.md).

## Security

- **A verifiable image.** The image is signed, and comes with a signed SBOM, build provenance and the scan results. See [Verify a release](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.60.0/guide/security-overview.md#verify-a-release) to check it before you deploy, and the [security overview](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.60.0/guide/security-overview.md) for what the app stores and connects to.
- **Sign-in is limited per address:** 60 sign-ins a minute from one address. The other limits are in the security overview.
- **AI agents** whose access has expired simply sign in again: an expired key doesn't count towards the limit on refused keys.
- **Dependencies** are updated for the release. A release is published only when its scan finds no high or critical vulnerability that has a fix, and the latest release is scanned again every week.

## Known issues

- Widening a card can leave other lines running over it ([#47](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/47)).
- A line between a Stakeholder circle and a card can have a small step where a straight line would fit ([#51](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/51)).

## Not there yet

- Some automatic line routes take long detours ([#39](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/39)).
- Tidy up in a view doesn't fit the result to the screen ([#57](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/57)).
- A role removed at the sign-in provider takes effect at the person's next sign-in, at most 8 hours later ([#26](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/26)).
- A database whose certificate comes from a private certificate authority can't be used yet ([#27](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/27)).
