# SimpleArchitecture 0.67.0

Lines between cards above and below each other leave and enter where the cards face each other.

**Still a beta.** Back up your database before you upgrade, and please [report what you find](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues).

## What changes

- **Lines use the sides where cards face each other.** A line between a card and one below it, off to the side, now runs from the bottom of the upper card to the top of the lower one, instead of coming into the side of a card. Cards as far or further apart sideways are joined from side to side. Only when another card or a container's name is in that way does a line take other sides. Lines moved by hand keep their route; **Tidy relations** draws them again the new way. See [The canvas](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.67.0/guide/canvas.md) ([#74](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/74)).

## Upgrade

There are no database changes: start 0.67.0 in place of the older version. See [Hosting the image](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.67.0/guide/hosting.md) for upgrading in general.

## Known issues

- Widening a card can leave other lines running over it ([#47](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/47)).
