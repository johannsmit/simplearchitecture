# SimpleArchitecture 0.66.0

Select several cards on the canvas and move them together.

**Still a beta.** Back up your database before you upgrade, and please [report what you find](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues).

## What changes

- **Move several cards at once.** `Shift`+click or `Ctrl`+click (`⌘`+click on a Mac) cards to add them to the selection, and again to take one out. Drag any of them and they all move together, keeping their places relative to each other. Each card stays in its own container or Group by box. This works on the explorer canvas and in a view; in a view in Modelling, the new places are saved. `Esc` keeps only the card shown in the details panel. See [The canvas](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.66.0/guide/canvas.md) ([#73](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/73)).

## Upgrade

There are no database changes: start 0.66.0 in place of the older version. See [Hosting the image](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.66.0/guide/hosting.md) for upgrading in general.

## Known issues

- Widening a card can leave other lines running over it ([#47](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/47)).
