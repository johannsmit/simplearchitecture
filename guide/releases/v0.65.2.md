# SimpleArchitecture 0.65.2

Lines between a Stakeholder circle and a card now run straight.

**Still a beta.** Back up your database before you upgrade, and please [report what you find](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues).

## What changes

- **Straight lines to and from a circle.** A line between a Stakeholder circle and a card level with it runs straight when there is room, after Tidy up and after Tidy relations, as lines between two cards already do. Before, it could have a small sideways step, in the middle of the line or next to the circle ([#51](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/51)).

## Upgrade

There are no database changes: start 0.65.2 in place of the older version. See [Hosting the image](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.65.2/guide/hosting.md) for upgrading in general.

## Known issues

- Widening a card can leave other lines running over it ([#47](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/47)).
