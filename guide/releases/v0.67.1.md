# SimpleArchitecture 0.67.1

Lines from a card inside a container cross the container's header beside its name.

**Still a beta.** Back up your database before you upgrade, and please [report what you find](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues).

## What changes

- **Lines cross a container's header beside its name.** A line from a card inside a container to a card above or below the container may cross the container's header beside its name, so it keeps to the top and bottom of the cards where they face each other, instead of leaving through the container's side. Only the container's name, with a little room around it, keeps lines away. See [The canvas](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.67.1/guide/canvas.md) ([#74](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/74)).

## Upgrade

There are no database changes: start 0.67.1 in place of the older version. See [Hosting the image](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.67.1/guide/hosting.md) for upgrading in general.

## Known issues

- Widening a card can leave other lines running over it ([#47](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/47)).
