# SimpleArchitecture 0.64.0

Lines that follow their cards now go round the cards in their way, instead of crossing them.

**Still a beta.** Back up your database before you upgrade, and please [report what you find](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues).

## What changes

- **Lines go round cards in their way.** When you move a card, a line without a saved route used to take the shortest way with at most two corners, and crossed a card between its two ends when that way had none free. It now takes more corners where it must, keeping a little room from the cards it passes and off the headers of containers and group boxes, with as few corners as it can. It crosses a card only when there is no way round, such as a card closed in by others. Lines that already found a free way are drawn as before. Lines to and from a Stakeholder circle still take the shortest way. See [The canvas](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.64.0/guide/canvas.md#layout-tidy-up-and-tidy-relations).

## Upgrade

There are no database changes: start 0.64.0 in place of the older version. See [Hosting the image](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.64.0/guide/hosting.md) for upgrading in general.

## Known issues

- The corners outside a Stakeholder's circle can still be clicked as part of the circle ([#63](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/63)).
- Widening a card can leave other lines running over it ([#47](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/47)).
- A line between a Stakeholder circle and a card can have a small step where a straight line would fit ([#51](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/51)).
