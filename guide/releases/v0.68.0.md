# SimpleArchitecture 0.68.0

Lines take the short way between their cards, and **Tidy up** is gone.

**Still a beta.** Back up your database before you upgrade, and please [report what you find](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues).

## What changes

- **Lines take the short way.** The canvas draws every line that isn't moved by hand itself, never along the automatic layout's own route. A line up from a card below to one above it, or into a card inside a container, no longer runs round every container: it takes the short right-angled way between the cards, with as few corners as it can. See [The canvas](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.68.0/guide/canvas.md) ([#39](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/39)).
- **Tidy up is removed.** The toolbar keeps **Tidy relations** (or `T`) as a button of its own: it draws every line again without moving a card. The canvas still lays itself out when it is new, such as when you open an element or a view. A Contains changed between cards already on the canvas is nested when the canvas is opened again.

## Upgrade

There are no database changes: start 0.68.0 in place of the older version. A view saved after **Tidy up** in an older version may still hold long lines: open it and choose **Tidy relations** once to draw them the short way. See [Hosting the image](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.68.0/guide/hosting.md) for upgrading in general.

## Known issues

- Widening a card can leave other lines running over it ([#47](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/47)).
