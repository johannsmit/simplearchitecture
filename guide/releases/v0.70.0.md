# SimpleArchitecture 0.70.0

Admin → Branding sets a product title, a logo for the Dark theme and an app icon.

**Still a beta.** Back up your database before you upgrade, and please [report what you find](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues).

## What changes

- **A product title.** Your own name for the app, in place of SimpleArchitecture: in the header, on the sign-in page, in the browser tab and as the installed app's name. Up to 40 characters on one line; leave it empty for SimpleArchitecture ([#28](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/28)).
- **A logo for the Dark theme.** An optional second logo, shown in the Dark theme. Without it, the Dark theme shows the logo as before. Switching the theme swaps it at once ([#28](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/28)).
- **An app icon.** A square PNG of 512 × 512 pixels for the browser tab, the home screen and the installed app. Without it, the built-in icon is used, as before ([#28](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/28)).
- **Reset to the neutral look** now also removes the product title, the Dark theme logo and the app icon.

See [The Admin space](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.70.0/guide/admin.md#branding).

## Upgrade

1. **Back up the database.** There is no downgrade: an older version refuses the upgraded database, so to go back, restore the backup and start the older version.
2. **Start 0.70.0.** The database change (three new, empty branding settings) runs automatically when the app starts, with nothing to do by hand.

See [Hosting the image](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/blob/v0.70.0/guide/hosting.md) for upgrading in general.

## Known issues

- Widening a card can leave other lines running over it ([#47](https://github.com/johannsmit/simplearchitecture/issues/47)).
- An app installed before the app icon changed may keep its old icon until the device refreshes it.
